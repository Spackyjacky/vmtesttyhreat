Who We Are

About Traceline Intelligence

A Cardiff-based specialist practice built around one principle: digital investigations done properly — methodologically rigorous, lawfully conducted, and documented to a standard that stands up.

The Background

Why Traceline Intelligence exists

Traceline Intelligence was built because there was a gap — and it was obvious once you started looking. Individuals in Wales who had been defrauded, harassed, or deceived had nowhere local to turn for digital investigation work done to a professional standard. Businesses dealing with internal fraud often had no specialist to help them build a documented case before calling their solicitors. Legal professionals looking for digital evidence support had to look outside Wales entirely, and often couldn't find what they actually needed: a structured OSINT report that would hold up under scrutiny.

The founder's background is in cybersecurity and security operations centre (SOC) analysis — the kind of work that involves tracking adversaries, identifying patterns in digital behaviour, and documenting findings in a form that is auditable and defensible. That background shapes everything about how Traceline operates.

The methodology is trained, not improvised. Traceline's approach is built around the frameworks developed by Bellingcat and IntelTechniques — the same frameworks used by investigative journalists, law enforcement researchers, and intelligence professionals worldwide. Applied to private investigation work, they produce something that generic PI agencies simply don't: investigations that are genuinely digital-native, and reports that are built around evidence rather than assertion.

The public speaker side of the practice matters too. Presenting on digital threats at BSides Birmingham — a respected information security conference — means engaging with an audience of professionals who understand both the power and the limits of digital intelligence. It keeps the methodology sharp and honest.

Traceline is not a large agency. It's a specialist practice. That means direct access to the investigator who handles your case, not a call handler passing messages. It means a clear answer about what we can and can't do. And it means that when you receive a report, you know exactly who wrote it and why.

How We Work

The Traceline approach

Methodology first. Evidence always collected lawfully. Every report built to withstand scrutiny. No shortcuts.

🔬

Methodology before tools

We follow structured OSINT methodology — not just running searches and hoping something appears. Every investigation has a defined scope, a collection plan, and an audit trail. The methodology is documented in the report so it can be examined and, if necessary, challenged.

⚖️

The law is the boundary

Open-source intelligence is powerful precisely because it's lawful. We don't use deception, we don't access systems without authorisation, and we don't do anything that would breach UK GDPR or the Computer Misuse Act. These aren't inconveniences — they're what makes the evidence admissible.

📋

Reports that survive scrutiny

A finding that can't be explained, attributed, and documented is just an assertion. Every Traceline report is written so that a reader who wasn't there — a judge, a barrister, an opposing expert — can follow exactly what was done and why it supports the conclusions drawn.

🤝

Honest about limits

There are things digital investigation can't tell you. If that's the case for your situation, we'll say so at the outset — not after you've paid. We'd rather turn down a case than produce a report that can't support the weight placed on it.

What We Stand For

Three things we won't compromise on

01
Lawful

We never cross the line. Every technique we use is open-source and lawful. If something isn't legal, we don't do it — regardless of what a client might want. This is non-negotiable, and it's what makes our reports usable.

02
Rigorous

Methodology that holds up. We don't make assertions we can't support. We don't produce conclusions that outrun the evidence. Every finding is documented, every source is recorded, and every report can be examined by someone who wasn't there.

03
Human

We understand what's at stake. People who come to us are often in difficult, stressful situations. We communicate clearly, we don't use jargon, and we make sure clients understand what we're doing and why. You're not a case number.
