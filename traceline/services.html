Report Quality

What every report includes

Regardless of service type, every Traceline investigation report is structured to the same standard — one that holds up wherever you need to take it.

✓

Methodology Statement

A clear account of how the investigation was conducted — what sources were used, what tools were applied, and in what sequence.

✓

Lawful Collection Confirmation

Explicit confirmation that all evidence was collected in accordance with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Computer Misuse Act 1990.

✓

Findings of Fact

A clear, objective account of what was found — stated as fact where verifiable, and clearly flagged where interpretation or inference is involved.

✓

Analysis

The investigator's assessment of what the findings mean in the context of the instruction — what they establish, what remains uncertain, and what further investigation might reveal.

✓

Statement of Truth

A signed statement confirming the accuracy of the report's contents — the standard required for use in legal proceedings.

✓

Exhibits & Appendices

All supporting evidence — screenshots, preserved web pages, metadata records, and source documentation — attached and indexed.
