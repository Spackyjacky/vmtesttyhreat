Why It Matters

The problem with most digital evidence

❌

Screenshots aren't evidence

A screenshot with no metadata, no preservation record, and no methodology behind it is easily challenged. Courts increasingly expect more. We provide the documented evidence chain that makes digital material usable.

❌

Unstructured reports don't hold up

An investigator who can't explain their methodology, identify their sources, or articulate the limits of their findings is a liability in cross-examination. Our reports are built to be examined — that's the point.

✓

OSINT fills critical gaps

Parties routinely conceal assets, misrepresent their circumstances, and maintain hidden online presences. Open-source intelligence finds what isn't disclosed — lawfully, and in a form that supports your case.

✓

A local specialist you can refer with confidence

Cardiff-based, accessible, and responsive. We understand the demands of legal practice — tight deadlines, precise instructions, and the need for a clear answer on what the evidence does and doesn't establish.

CPR Part 35 awareness: Our reports are structured with Part 35 in mind — the expert's duty to the court is reflected in how findings are framed, how uncertainty is disclosed, and how the limits of open-source intelligence are stated. We are not a retained expert for the instructing party; we are a factual investigator. That distinction is made clear in the report.
