What we do

Decorating services for every job, large or small

Whether it's a single room refresh or a full commercial respray, JWDC covers the full range of decorating and coating work.

Domestic Decorating

Interior & exterior painting, feature walls, ceilings, woodwork and trim — finished to a standard you'd be happy to live with.

Domestic & Commercial Spraying

Airless spray finishes for doors, kitchens, furniture, walls, ceilings and commercial units — a faster, smoother finish than brush and roller.

Commercial Decorating

Offices, retail units, communal areas and new-builds, scheduled around your business with minimal disruption.

Wallpapering & Wall Coverings

Pattern-matched wallpaper hanging, feature walls and specialist wall coverings, hung neatly and precisely.

Plastering Prep & Surface Repairs

Filling, sanding, caulking and surface preparation — the unglamorous work that makes for a flawless finish.

Exterior Painting & Masonry Coatings

Weatherproof masonry paint, render, fascias, soffits and guttering — protecting the property as well as improving it.

Kitchen & Furniture Respraying

Transform kitchen cabinets, doors and furniture with a durable sprayed finish, without the cost of replacing them.

Fire Doors & Radiator Spraying

Compliant fire-door finishing and radiator/pipework spraying for landlords, letting agents and facilities managers.

Restoration & Specialist Finishes

Wood staining, varnishing, and specialist decorative finishes to restore and refresh tired surfaces.

Don't see what you need listed? If it's decorating or coatings related, we've likely got it covered — get in touch and ask.

About JWDC

Local decorators, based right here in Splott

J Wise Decorators & Coatings is a Cardiff decorating company based in Splott, working across the city and the wider South Wales area. We take on domestic jobs — a single room, a full house repaint, wallpapering — through to commercial spraying contracts for offices, retail units and landlords.

Every job gets the same approach: proper preparation, a tidy site, and a finish that's built to last, not just look good on the day we leave.
